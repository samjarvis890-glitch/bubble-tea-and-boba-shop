FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

HERE FOR YOUR BOBA QUESTIONS

Curious about ingredients, custom sweetness, party catering, or allergens? We have you covered.

We believe everyone deserves boba happiness! We offer certified organic oat milk, creamy soy milk, and rich coconut milk with zero dairy. In addition, all of our fruit teas, jasmine green teas, crystal boba, and coconut jellies are 100% vegan. Explore dairy-free choices on our Customizer Page.
Yes, absolutely! Every single cup is shaken to your personal preference. Choose from five sweetness levels (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and four ice levels (No Ice, Light Ice, Regular Ice, Extra Chill). You can try building your recipe in our interactive Drink Customizer.
We love celebrating milestones with you! We cater gatherings of 20 to 250+ guests with sealed fresh cups and topping flight bars. Simply select "Party Catering & Private Events" on our Contact Form above with your date and approximate guest count.
Transparency matters deeply to us. You can find allergen badges (dairy, gluten, nuts, caffeine) on our Menu Page. If you have specific dietary sensitivities, our team is always pleased to customize recipes without cross-contact.
Our first flagship location is under construction in downtown. You can view our planned hours and amenities in the Store Locator section above, and subscribe for opening day perks and complimentary boba giveaways.
We love fan creativity! Many seasonal drinks begin with suggestions from our community. Select "Recipe Suggestion & Sweet Feedback" in our Contact Form or tag @bobabloomtea on social media.
